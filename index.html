Jury Presentation Scenario (15-Step Flow)

Follow this sequence during your pitch to showcase full real-time synchronization between Teacher and Student perspectives:

  1. Start in Teacher View — Select Engineering Mathematics (10:00 AM).
  2. Click 🚶 ON MY WAY — Broadcast is instantly dispatched.
  3. Click 🎓 Student View in the top bar.
  4. Notice the Audio Chime and the focal hero card displaying 🟢 Teacher On The Way.
  5. Click the primary button: ✓ I'M SEATED IN CLASS.
  6. Notice the button transforms into ✓ SEATED with an instant feedback toast.
  7. Click 👨‍🏫 Teacher View.
  8. Notice the Live Presence Gauge immediately updated from 27 → 28 seated students.
  9. In Teacher View, click ⏱ DELAY → +10 min.
  10. Notice the animated countdown starts (e.g. 09:59).
  11. Switch to 🎓 Student View — see the live countdown banner synchronizing second-by-second.
  12. Switch to 👨‍🏫 Teacher View and click 🚪 ROOM SHIFT (change AB-204 → AB-305).
  13. Switch to 🎓 Student View — see the pulsating ROOM CHANGED TO AB-305 banner.
  14. Switch back to Teacher and click ❌ CANCEL CLASS.
  15. Confirm in the modal to see the immediate 🔴 CLASS CANCELLED broadcast!
